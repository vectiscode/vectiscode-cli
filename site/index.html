Usage doctor for Claude Code & Codex

Where did your limits go?

vectiscode reads the logs Claude Code and Codex already keep on your computer, finds what drains your limits, and tells you what to change.

npx vectiscode

Runs locally and read-only. Nothing leaves your computer. Needs Node 22 or newer.

Five checks, each with a fix

  1. Long chatsEvery step re-reads the whole chat, so late steps in a long chat cost several times what early ones do.
  2. Always-on contextTool definitions, MCP servers, skills and CLAUDE.md go out with every request, used or not.
  3. Cache after breaksCome back after the cache has expired and the next step pays to write the whole chat again.
  4. Helpers and subagentsEach one carries its own context, so see what share of your usage they take.
  5. Codex limitsHow much of your 5-hour and weekly limits each chat and project used.

How the numbers work

Claude CodeEvery request's tokens are weighed at API list prices, checked against Claude Code's own cost records, and shown as units: a fair scale for comparing chats and habits, not your bill.

CodexCodex logs how much of your 5-hour and weekly limits you have used after every turn. vectiscode credits each turn with the change, so these are real percentages, not estimates.
